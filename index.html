Selected projects

01

Kamaee

An offline-first app that turns an Android phone into a shopkeeper’s counter. It works without internet, extra hardware or a monthly fee. Sales, inventory, bulk restocking, cash and part-paid payments, khata, returns, receipts and profit tracking all run locally in SQLite. The app can also send daily reports, bills and payment reminders through WhatsApp.

  • Theft detectionI designed a way to catch a type of theft that normal sales records cannot see: when someone takes an item from the shelf, sells it, pockets the cash and never records the sale. The books still balance because there is no transaction to reconcile. The approach is to compare physical shelf counts with the stock that should be there based on transaction history.
  • AI guardrailsThe WhatsApp assistant lets shopkeepers ask questions about their business in Urdu or Roman Urdu. Instead of relying on prompts to keep the model safe, I constrained what it can actually do. It chooses from a fixed set of typed tools, including an explicit refusal option, and the shop identity is resolved before any tool runs. The model also never calculates numbers. Tested application code computes every figure first and syncs the results to Firestore; the model only turns those results into a response.
Flutter · Dart · SQLite · Firestore · Claude API · ~17K lines · CI-gated

02

Smart Notifications at Motive

A real-time fuel recommendation system for truck drivers at Motive (formerly KeepTruckin). It uses live GPS, speed and tank-level telemetry to detect when a driver needs fuel and recommends stations based on remaining range, fuel price, fleet-card discounts, driving distance and road detour. A second flow uses upcoming HOS mandatory breaks to suggest suitable places to stop.

  • Data & infrastructureI integrated data across 5–6 services, including vehicle telemetry, directions, fleet cards and OSRM for driving distance and ETA. I also built the pipeline for fuel-station locations and partner discounts from networks such as Love’s and TA, ingesting SFTP/FTP files through SQS and parallel background workers that processed large feeds in chunks without locking the pipeline. The recommendation service handled around 190K requests per 5 seconds (~38K RPS), including complex rule evaluation.
  • Recommendation engineI built a configurable rules engine that combined detour, remaining range, price, discounts and other product constraints, allowing the recommendation logic to change without rewriting the system. I also conducted a literature study on US road-network structure and detour indices to understand how actual driving distance differs from simple geographic distance. One practical challenge was noisy GPS bearing while trucks were moving, so I used circular means and other smoothing techniques to prevent recommendations from jumping between directions.
  • End to endI owned the feature from research and product design through frontend, backend, deployments, observability and iteration. The system increased fleet-card usage by 14% among eligible drivers and was launched as a flagship feature at Motive’s 2026 Product Launch Summit.
Rails · Go microservices · RPC · OSRM · Angular · PostgreSQL · Kafka · SQS
